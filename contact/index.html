Reporting a calculator issue

  • Describe what you expected and what actually happened.
  • Include your browser/device and the page URL.
  • Use made-up example income and expense amounts to reproduce the problem.
  • For a tax-content correction, include the relevant tax year and an IRS or SSA reference.
Keep personal information private.

Do not post Social Security numbers, tax returns, account credentials, identification documents, or your actual financial details. GitHub posts may be public.
