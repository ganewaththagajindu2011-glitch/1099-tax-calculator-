Privacy, without the guesswork

Last updated: October 11, 2026

DGA family operates ClearTax. This notice describes the calculator code and pages published at this website. It distinguishes your calculator inputs from the technical information involved in loading a hosted website.

Your calculator inputs

The income, business expense, and filing-status values you enter are processed in your browser. The calculator code does not submit these values or calculated tax results to a server, place them in URLs, or save them to cookies, local storage, or a project database.

Your browser or device may retain form values through its own autofill or session-restoration features. Those features are controlled by your browser. The calculator does not require an account, uploaded documents, or payment information.

Cookies and analytics

The published application code does not set cookies or include advertising pixels, Google Analytics, or other analytics scripts. This statement concerns the application code, not browser extensions or services outside the site’s control. If analytics or other data-collection features are introduced, this notice should be updated to describe them.

Hosting and technical request information

The site is hosted on Vercel. Loading any hosted page sends technical request information, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time, to the hosting infrastructure. Vercel may process this information for delivery, security, and service operation. We do not specify a retention period for provider-controlled logs here.

Review Vercel’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab) for provider practices. These page requests are separate from the financial values processed by the calculator.

Information you send by email

If you contact DGA family at ganewaththagajindu2011@gmail.com, your email address, message, and any attachments are delivered through email providers and may be retained in correspondence to respond to your request, troubleshoot a reported problem, or handle a privacy concern. Only send information needed for your request; do not send tax returns, Social Security numbers, passwords, or financial account details.

Email is separate from the calculator: entering values in the calculator does not send an email. For a question about your correspondence or a deletion request, contact the same address. Provider-controlled records may follow separate retention practices.

External links and public support

Links to the IRS, SSA, GitHub, or another external website take you to that service, whose own policies apply. If you choose to contact the maintainer through GitHub, the information you post may be public. Do not include tax returns, Social Security numbers, passwords, or actual financial details.

Questions and updates

Use the project contact options on the Contact page for a site-related privacy concern. This notice will need revision if the site’s data practices change; the date above identifies this version.
