ClearTax · Independent tax planning

A clearer starting point for freelance taxes

ClearTax is a free browser-based planning tool for US freelancers, independent contractors, and gig workers. It turns annual income and business expenses into an estimate of federal income tax, regular self-employment tax, and monthly or quarterly savings targets.

Simple to use

No account or tax documents required. Adjust your inputs and see the estimate update.

How the estimate works

  1. Subtract entered business expenses from gross freelance revenue to estimate net profit.
  2. Calculate regular self-employment tax on 92.35% of positive net profit, applying the earnings threshold and Social Security cap.
  3. Estimate federal income tax using half of regular SE tax as an adjustment, the basic standard deduction, and 2026 progressive brackets for your filing status.
  4. Divide estimated annual tax by 12 or 4 to show budgeting targets.

See the formula breakdown, business deduction guide, and quarterly payment guide.

What this calculator does not cover

This is a simplified estimate for freelance income alone. It does not include state or local taxes, W-2 or spouse income, tax credits, QBI, Additional Medicare Tax, itemized deductions, or several other individual adjustments. Its savings targets are not IRS-required installments.

ClearTax is not a tax filing service or an IRS service. The calculator and guides have not received a qualified tax professional’s sign-off. Use a qualified professional for advice specific to your return.

Sources and corrections
